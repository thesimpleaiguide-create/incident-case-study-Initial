4 Critical Safeguarding Breaches

Tap to uncover the clinical and governance breaches against NHS England standards.

1

Video Care Chaperones

Breach: Remote care assumed exempt.

Under NHS England Dec 2025 guidance, visual intimate inspections via video require the exact same proactive chaperone offer as clinic visits.

2

Privacy Assessment

Breach: No setting verification.

Clinicians must actively confirm the patient is in a secure, confidential room before asking for clothing adjustments.

3

Alternative Pathways

Breach: Patient left without options.

Patients must always be offered face-to-face appointments with a chaperone or secure asynchronous photo submission.

4

EHR Documentation

Breach: Indefensible note.

Audit-ready EHR entries must document the chaperone offer, patient decision, chaperone identity/role, and explicit consent.
